# Regulations & Policies

Banks in India are regulated by the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**. Key rules relevant to loan decisions:

## 1. Fair Practices Code for Lenders
- Lenders must be transparent about interest rates, fees and terms.
- If a loan application is rejected, the lender should convey the main reasons in writing.

## 2. KYC (Know Your Customer) Norms
- Every borrower's identity and address must be verified using officially valid documents (e.g. Aadhaar, passport, voter ID, driving licence) and PAN.

## 3. Loan-to-Value (LTV) Limits
- For **housing loans**, RBI caps how much of the property value a bank can lend, with lower limits for bigger loans.
- For **gold loans**, RBI also caps the loan amount as a % of the gold's value.
- *Verify the current limits on the RBI website before final submission.*

## 4. Credit Information
- Banks report borrowers' repayment data to credit bureaus (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF High Mark), which produce credit reports and scores.

## 5. NPA Classification
- A loan becomes a Non-Performing Asset if interest or principal is overdue for more than 90 days.
