# Credit Score Knowledge

## What is a credit score?
A credit score is a 3-digit number between **300 and 900** that shows how reliably a person has repaid loans and credit card bills in the past. In India, the most commonly used score is from **TransUnion CIBIL**. Other credit bureaus include Experian, Equifax and CRIF High Mark.

## Score bands (general guide – banks set their own cut-offs)
| Score | General meaning | Usual effect on loan |
|---|---|---|
| 750 – 900 | Excellent / Good | High chance of approval, better interest rate |
| 700 – 749 | Fair | Usually approved, possibly with conditions |
| 600 – 699 | Average / Risky | Needs collateral or manager review |
| Below 600 | Poor | Usually rejected |
| NH / NA | No credit history | Needs extra checks (new to credit) |

## What improves a score
- Paying EMIs and credit card bills on time
- Keeping credit card usage low compared to the limit
- Having a healthy mix of loans (secured and unsecured)
- Keeping old accounts open for a long history

## What hurts a score
- Late or missed payments
- Loans written off or settled
- Applying for many loans in a short time
- Using most of the credit card limit every month
